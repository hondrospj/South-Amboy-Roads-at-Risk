Sources and coverage

South Amboy, NJ

Prepared September 9, 2026. South Amboy municipal terrain and road coverage. The continuous gauge is across the water at Perth Amboy; forecast point est0007 and historical storm-crest station 01406710 represent South Amboy.

  • USGS 01396060 — Arthur Kill at Perth Amboy (nearby gauge for South Amboy): observations in feet NAVD88, parameter 72279. The local continuous archive starts 2016-08-19; this snapshot extends through 2026-09-09. Live readings refresh on request. Gaps longer than 18 minutes remain missing in the newly rebuilt record. Older retained records may use their original interpolation methods.
  • NOAA P-ETSS, point est0007 (South Amboy guidance). The model cycle is shown on the forecast. Low and high guidance are the 90% and 10% exceedance products. Nearby forecast and observation locations can differ. The retained local datum conversion is NAVD88 = MLLW -3.06 ft; applying this offset to nearby forecast guidance is an approximate spatial transfer.
  • Local screening thresholds: 4.14, 5.14, 6.14 ft NAVD88 for minor, moderate and major flooding. These are the existing town configuration.
  • RoadRisk terrain: USGS 3DEP Bare Earth DEM Dynamic ImageServer. 5-ft Web Mercator grid, elevations in meters converted to feet. Grid spacing is not measurement accuracy. The flood overlay and road profile can use different grid spacing or processing.
  • NJ road centerlines and NJ municipal boundaries, clipped to the selected town.
  • Earlier event-only records come from USGS storm-tide crest station 01406710, Raritan River at South Amboy, NJ. These are discrete crests, not a continuous historical water-level series at the modern gauge.
  • Pre-gauge proxy history, where selected, comes from Sandy Hook, New Jersey, 8531680. It is regional proxy history and should not be interpreted as measurements at this municipality.
  • South Amboy’s existing 5-ft connected flood catalog is retained, including its local stage settings and low-stage depth adjustment. This refresh changes the interface and data access, not a new hydraulic calibration.

Interpret the maps

These are tidal flood screening tools. They do not simulate rainfall, drainage, waves, flow velocities or structure failures. Bare-earth terrain can differ from elevated roads and bridge decks. A dry cell does not establish safe access or safe driving.

RoadRisk frequency curves are conditional scenarios that shift the observed water-level distribution, not predictions of individual storms. Regional sea-level trend and scenario sources are shown in the dashboard. Its older regional annual counts remain regional data; they are not a complete long record of local floods. Partial years and missing observations affect historical comparisons. The adjustable ±0.5 ft band is a sensitivity setting, not a certified accuracy bound.

The historical archive and RoadRisk baseline are September 2026 snapshots. Recent observations and forecasts refresh on request; later gaps in the permanent archive require a rebuild. Unavailable live guidance is shown as unavailable.
